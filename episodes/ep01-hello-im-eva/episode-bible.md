## 1. WHAT THIS EPISODE IS FOR

The pilot. Its job is **attachment, not teaching.** A FUN episode carries no learning objective and
must not be given one.

1. **Make a child love Eva.** Everything else is secondary.
2. **Establish the contract** — *you are not watching Eva, you are going with her.*
3. **Introduce the Adventure Star** as the show's ritual object.
4. **Introduce the other three**, each with their signature move.

**Logline:** Eva wants to show her friends her Adventure Star — but it's not where she left it, and
only the children watching saw where it went.

**The spine:** the child sees Danny take the star at **2:05**. Eva doesn't find out until **2:30**.
Twenty-five seconds where they are the only one who knows. They will shout at the screen. That
shouting is the product.

Per master bible rule 4, nothing is lost or broken. Danny hides the star and hands it back himself.

## 3. SCRIPT

### BEAT 1 — COLD OPEN (0:00–0:20)

*Eva's Room, early morning. Warm low light. Very quiet — one bird outside.*

*On the shelf, the Adventure Star gives one slow warm pulse and a single soft chime. Eva's ears
twitch. She lifts her head.*

> **EVA:** *(sleepy, almost a whisper)* …Did you see that?

*She looks straight down the lens. Beat. Her face opens.*

> **EVA:** I think today is going to be a **good** day.

**Direction:** open quietly. A whisper makes a child lean in. Never open loud.

### BEAT 2 — TITLES (0:20–0:45)

*Theme — [theme-v2b-call-response.mp3](../../audio/theme-v2b-call-response.mp3).*

On each **"Who's that?"** a character card lands; on each **"BA-BY E-VA!"** Eva lands. All four
faces established before the story starts.

### BEAT 3 — INTRODUCTION (0:45–1:15)

> **EVA:** Hello! I'm Eva.
> *(beat — she waits, blinking, pleased you came)*
> **EVA:** Look — this is my **Adventure Star.**

*Push in on the star, resting on the shelf.*

> **EVA:** It sleeps on my shelf… and when it **glows**, it means an adventure is starting!

**Direction:** "Adventure Star" said slowly and clearly. A child is hearing the name of an object
they will see in every episode for years.

### BEAT 4 — PARTICIPATION (1:15–1:40)

> **EVA:** Can you wave hello?
> **[PAUSE — 3s. Eva on screen, facing lens. Music thins to one held note.]**
> **EVA:** *(delighted)* I saw that! Hello!

*She lifts the star down, both paws, careful.*

> **EVA:** I want to show my star to my friends. Are you ready?
> **[PAUSE — 3s]**
> **EVA:** Then come on, little adventurers!

### BEAT 5 — ADVENTURE (1:40–2:25)

*The Garden. Golden morning, felt flowers, low wooden fence, butterflies.*

*LITTLE BUNNY bursts in, hopping — whole-body joy.*

> **LITTLE BUNNY:** Eva! You're here!
> **EVA:** *(to lens)* This is my best friend. Can you say hello to Little Bunny?
> **[PAUSE — 3s]**

*MR RABIT at the fence, kneeling to their height.*

> **MR RABIT:** Good morning, you two. And good morning to all our friends watching.

*Eva sets the star carefully on the low wall.*

> **EVA:** I'll just put you there for a moment.

**★ 2:05 — THE "THEY KNOW FIRST" MOMENT.** *Eva and Bunny turn to watch a butterfly. Behind them
DANNY tiptoes in on enormous feet, hiding behind a flowerpot far too small for him, one spike
snagging a leaf. He looks at the star. He looks at camera. He grins — conspiratorial, never
menacing — and puts a huge finger to his lips. He takes it and creeps off, badly.*

*Hold 3 full seconds on the empty wall. Danny's comic cue plays. Funny, not ominous.*

### BEAT 6 — CHALLENGE (2:25–3:00)

> **EVA:** Little Bunny, come and see my Adventure St—

*She stops. The wall is empty. Ears drop. Brows go **up**, not down.*

> **EVA:** …Oh. Where is it?

**Critical:** Eva is **puzzled, not distressed.** No crying, no panic. A toddler reads fear
instantly and this is the pilot.

> **LITTLE BUNNY:** It was right **here**!
> **EVA:** *(to lens)* Did you see where it went?
> **[PAUSE — 3s]**
> **EVA:** *(small gasp)* Uh-oh… **Danny!**

*Wrong guess 1 — under the fence. Nothing. Wrong guess 2 — in the flowers. Nothing.*

> **EVA:** *(small)* …It's not there either.

*She sits on the grass. Thinking, not sad. Mr Rabit kneels beside her.*

### BEAT 7 — THINKING MOMENT (3:00–3:30)

> **MR RABIT:** When something is lost, Eva… what do we do?
> **MR RABIT:** We **stop**… *(open palm, held)*
> **MR RABIT:** …we **think**… *(finger to temple, held)*
> **MR RABIT:** …and we **look!** *(hand shading eyes, held)*
> **MR RABIT:** Can you do it with me?
> **[PAUSE — 3s. He performs it once more, slowly, so children can copy.]**

**Direction:** one gesture per word, each held long enough for a two-year-old to catch up. The
show's catechism, performed for the first time. Mr Rabit does **not** know where the star is and
must not appear to.

> **EVA:** *(to lens, close, hopeful)* Can you help me? Where should we look?
> **[PAUSE — 4s. No cut.]**

### BEAT 8 — THE SEARCH (3:30–4:05)

> **EVA:** Behind the flowerpot? Let's look together!

*Warm and playful, never tense. They check the watering can — empty.*

> **EVA:** Can you see something green?
> **[PAUSE — 3s]**

*Behind the flowerpot, plainly visible to the audience: a green tail and a row of purple spikes.
Danny is hiding extremely badly.*

> **LITTLE BUNNY:** *(whispering, thrilled)* Eva… I think it's **wiggling**.
> **EVA:** *(whisper, to lens)* Should we look behind it? One… two… **three!**

### BEAT 9 — SOLUTION (4:05–4:35)

*DANNY — enormous, entirely visible, crouched behind a pot a quarter his size, holding the star. He
freezes.*

> **DANNY:** *(sheepish, scuffing one huge foot)* …Hello.
> **DANNY:** *(quieter, not looking up)* I just wanted you to come and find me.

**The most important line in the episode.** It states Danny's motive on screen, in his own voice, in
his first appearance — and it is his character forever. Play it small and true. **No music under
it.**

> **EVA:** *(no scolding — delighted)* You could have just **asked**! Do you want to play with us?
> **DANNY:** *(whole body lifting)* **Really?**

*Danny holds out the star in both hands and gives it back himself. Warm sparkle bloom. The star
pulses… then **glows**.*

### BEAT 10 — REINFORCEMENT + GOODBYE (4:35–5:00)

> **EVA:** We did it — **together!**
> **LITTLE BUNNY:** Together!
> **DANNY:** *(shyly)* …Together?
> **EVA:** We couldn't have done it **without you.**
> **[PAUSE — 3s]**

*Cosy Corner. Lamp on, warm and low. Eva with the star in her lap, its glow fading to a sleepy dim.*

> **EVA:** Will you come back and have another adventure with me?
> **[PAUSE — 3s]**
> **EVA:** *(softly)* I hoped you'd say that.
> **EVA:** Bye bye, little adventurers!

*Star placed on the shelf. Dims to sleep. Warm fade.*

**Button:** *"Adventure starts with YOU!"*

## 4. INTERACTIVE MOMENTS

Eight, inside the 7–9 budget, with no gap longer than ~40 seconds.

| # | Time | Question | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:10 | "Did you see that?" | Hook |
| 2 | 1:18 | "Can you wave hello?" | Physical |
| 3 | 1:32 | "Are you ready?" | Call |
| 4 | 1:52 | "Can you say hello to Little Bunny?" | Social |
| 5 | 2:38 | "Did you see where it went?" | **The big one** |
| 6 | 3:18 | "Can you do it with me?" (Stop… Think… Look!) | Physical + method |
| 7 | 3:25 | "Can you help me? Where should we look?" | **The biggest one** |
| 8 | 3:50 | "Can you see something green?" | Spotting |
| 9 | 4:50 | "Will you come back?" | Relationship |

Moments 5 and 7 are the episode. If either is rushed in the edit, the episode has failed regardless
of how good the rest looks.

**Key phrase, three uses:** 1:38 the call · 3:32 "Let's look together!" · 4:57 the sign-off.

## 5. CONSISTENCY CHECKS

- [ ] Eva wears the pink heart backpack in **every** shot
- [ ] **No whiskers on Eva in any frame**; **no visible teeth on anyone but Danny**
- [ ] Danny visibly biggest; Little Bunny visibly smallest
- [ ] Mr Rabit and Little Bunny never side by side at matched scale
- [ ] Little Bunny's bow on her **left** ear
- [ ] Danny's spikes soft-tipped; grin never a snarl
- [ ] Camera at character eye level in every shot
- [ ] Eva's face ≥25% of frame height in direct address
- [ ] Every question gets its full pause, no cut during it
- [ ] Danny gives the star back himself
- [ ] Ends calm — last 25 seconds wind down
