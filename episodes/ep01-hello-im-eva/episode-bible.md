# EPISODE 1 — "Hello, I'm Eva!"

**Draft · Season 1, Episode 1 · FUN episode (odd) · 11:00 · 16:9**

Governed by [master-bible.md](../../bible/master-bible.md) v1.1 and
[visual-bible.md](../../bible/visual-bible.md) v1.1. Where this document disagrees with either,
they win.

## 1. WHAT THIS EPISODE IS FOR

This is the pilot, and its job is **attachment, not teaching.** As a FUN episode it carries no
learning objective and must not be given one.

Four jobs, in priority order:

1. **Make a child love Eva.** Everything else is secondary.
2. **Establish the contract** — *you are not watching Eva, you are going with her.* The child is
   addressed, asked, waited for and thanked, within the first 90 seconds.
3. **Introduce the Adventure Star** as the show's ritual object.
4. **Introduce the other three** — Little Bunny, Mr Rabit, Danny — each with their signature move,
   so a returning child recognises them instantly in episode 2.

**Logline:** Eva wants to show her friends her Adventure Star — but it's not where she left it, and
only the children watching saw where it went.

## 2. WHY THIS STORY FOR EPISODE 1

The star goes missing in the very first episode because that is the fastest way to make an object
matter. A child learns what the Adventure Star *is* by watching everyone care that it's gone.

It also establishes the show's central pleasure on day one: **the audience knows before Eva does.**
The child sees Danny take the star at **3:40**. Eva doesn't find out until **4:35**. Nearly a full
minute — including an entire happy song during which the child can see the empty wall behind the
dancing — where they are the only one in the room who knows. They will shout. That shouting is the
product.

Per master bible rule 4 nothing is lost or broken: Danny hides the star, he does not damage it, and
he hands it back himself.

**At 11:00 the extra time goes into dwelling, not plot.** More garden, more play, more looking. The
story is exactly as simple as the 3-minute version was. That is deliberate.

## 4. FULL SCRIPT

### BEAT 1 — COLD OPEN (0:00–0:25)

*Eva's Room, early morning. Warm low light through yellow curtains. Quiet. A soft ambient bed — one
bird outside, the room breathing.*

*On the shelf, the Adventure Star sits dim. It gives one slow warm **pulse**, and a single soft
chime.*

*Eva is curled asleep on the rug. Her ears twitch. She lifts her head.*

> **EVA:** *(sleepy, wondering, almost a whisper)* …Did you see that?

*She looks straight down the lens. Beat. Her face opens into delight.*

> **EVA:** I think today is going to be a **good** day.

*The star pulses again. Cut to titles on the pulse.*

**Direction:** the whisper is important. Opening quietly makes a child lean in. Do not open loud.

---

### BEAT 2 — TITLES (0:25–1:00)

*Theme song — [theme-v2b-call-response.mp3](../../audio/theme-v2b-call-response.mp3). Logo.*

Visuals cut to the song's call-and-response: on each **"Who's that?"** a character pops into frame;
on each **"BA-BY E-VA!"** Eva appears. Establishes all four faces before the story starts.

---

### BEAT 3 — INTRODUCTION (1:00–1:45)

*Eva's Room, now full warm morning. Eva centred, close, eye level.*

> **EVA:** Hello! I'm Eva.
> *(beat — she waits, blinking, genuinely pleased you came)*
> **EVA:** I'm so happy you're here today.

*She turns. Camera follows her look to the shelf.*

> **EVA:** Look — this is my **Adventure Star.**

*Push in gently on the star. It is soft, plush, yellow, resting.*

> **EVA:** It's the most special thing I have. It sleeps on my shelf…
> **EVA:** …and when it **glows**, it means an adventure is starting!

*She lifts it down carefully, both paws, like something precious.*

> **EVA:** Can you see it? Isn't it beautiful?
> **[PAUSE — 3s]**
> **EVA:** I think so too.

**Direction:** "Adventure Star" is said slowly and clearly. This is the first time a child hears the
name of an object they will see in every episode for years. Do not rush it.

---

### BEAT 4 — PARTICIPATION (1:45–2:30)

> **EVA:** Before we go… can you wave hello?
> **[PAUSE — 3s. Eva on screen, facing lens, expectant. Music thins to one held note.]**
> **EVA:** *(delighted)* I saw that! Hello!

*She hugs the star to her chest.*

> **EVA:** I want to show my star to **all** my friends today. Will you come with me?
> **[PAUSE — 3s]**
> **EVA:** Really? Oh, I'm so glad.

*She turns toward the door, then back — checking you're still there.*

> **EVA:** Are you ready?
> **[PAUSE — 2.5s]**
> **EVA:** *(bright, rising)* Then come on, little adventurers! Let's go outside!

---

### BEAT 5 — SEGMENT A: ADVENTURE (2:30–4:00)

*The Garden. Soft grass, oversized felt flowers, terracotta pots, a low wooden fence. Golden
morning. Butterflies.*

*Eva steps out, star in both paws, taking it in.*

> **EVA:** *(a happy sigh)* Ooh… everything looks so **new** in the morning.

*A rustle. **LITTLE BUNNY** bursts in, hopping — her whole body expressing joy.*

> **LITTLE BUNNY:** Eva! Eva! You're here!
> **EVA:** Little Bunny! *(to lens)* This is my best friend. Can you say hello to Little Bunny?
> **[PAUSE — 3s]**
> **LITTLE BUNNY:** *(bouncing, thrilled)* They said hello to **me**!

*At the low fence, **MR RABIT**, tall and calm in his spectacles and green jacket. He kneels to
their height.*

> **MR RABIT:** Good morning, you two. And good morning to all our friends watching.
> **EVA:** Mr Rabit! Look what I brought!
> **MR RABIT:** *(warm, unhurried)* Ah. The Adventure Star. What a fine thing to bring.

*Eva sets the star carefully on the low garden wall so she can use both paws.*

> **EVA:** I'll just put you there for a moment.

*Play. Little Bunny hops between the flowers. A butterfly loops past.*

> **EVA:** Can you see the butterfly?
> **[PAUSE — 3s]**
> **EVA:** There it goes!

**★ THE "THEY KNOW FIRST" MOMENT — 3:40.**

*Eva and Bunny are turned away, watching the butterfly. Behind them, **DANNY** tiptoes in on
enormous feet, hiding behind a flowerpot far too small for him, one dorsal spike snagging a leaf.*

*He looks at the star. He looks at the camera. He grins — delighted, conspiratorial, not remotely
menacing — and puts one huge finger to his lips.*

*He takes the star and creeps away, badly.*

**Hold for a full 3 seconds after he's gone, on the empty wall.** No music sting that warns Eva.
Danny's comic cue plays, and it is funny, not ominous.

---

### BEAT 6 — SONG BREAK 1 (4:00–4:30)

*Clap-along song — [theme-v2c-clap-along.mp3](../../audio/theme-v2c-clap-along.mp3).*

*Eva, Little Bunny and Mr Rabit clap, stomp and hop in the garden. Pure joy, no story.*

> **EVA:** Can you clap with me?

**Staging note — this is the best joke in the episode:** keep the **empty wall in frame**, softly,
behind the dancing. The child can see the star is gone for the entire song while everyone on screen
is happily oblivious. Do not cut away from it.

---

### BEAT 7 — SEGMENT B: CHALLENGE (4:30–6:00)

*Song ends. Eva turns to the wall, beaming.*

> **EVA:** Little Bunny, come and see my Adventure St—

*She stops. The wall is empty. Her ears drop. Her brows go **up**, not down.*

> **EVA:** …Oh.
> **EVA:** Where is it?

**Critical direction:** Eva is **puzzled, not distressed.** She does not cry, does not panic. A
toddler reads fear instantly and this is the pilot. Her voice stays soft and curious.

> **LITTLE BUNNY:** It was right **here**! I saw it!
> **EVA:** *(turning to the lens, remembering you're there)* Did you see where it went?
> **[PAUSE — 3s]**
> **EVA:** *(a small gasp)* Uh-oh… **Danny!**

**Eva's wrong guesses — required by master bible rule 5. She gets it wrong twice before the method
is offered, never after.**

> **EVA:** Maybe it rolled under the fence…

*She looks. Nothing. Little Bunny hops over to help.*

> **LITTLE BUNNY:** Not here!
> **EVA:** Maybe it's hiding in the flowers…

*Both look. A butterfly, no star.*

> **EVA:** *(small, to herself)* …It's not there either.

*She sits down on the grass. Not sad — thinking. Mr Rabit approaches and kneels.*

---

### BEAT 8 — THINKING MOMENT (6:00–6:45)

> **MR RABIT:** Eva. When something is lost… what do we do?
> **EVA:** *(looking up)* I don't know.
> **MR RABIT:** *(warm, delighted to be asked)* We **stop**…

*Open palm. Held.*

> **MR RABIT:** …we **think**…

*Finger to temple. Held.*

> **MR RABIT:** …and we **look!**

*Hand shading eyes. Held.*

> **MR RABIT:** Can you do it with me? Stop… Think… Look!
> **[PAUSE — 4s. Mr Rabit performs it once more, slowly, so children can copy.]**

**Direction:** one gesture per word, each held long enough for a two-year-old to catch up. This is
the show's catechism and this is its first performance. Get it right here and it is right for
twenty episodes. Mr Rabit does **not** know where the star is and must not appear to.

*Eva stands, straightens, turns to the lens — close, low, hopeful.*

> **EVA:** Can you help me? Where should we look?
> **[PAUSE — 4s. No cut. Eva leans in slightly, listening.]**
> **EVA:** *(lighting up)* Behind the flowerpot? Let's look together!

---

### BEAT 9 — SEGMENT C: THE SEARCH (6:45–8:30)

*The search. Warm, playful, never tense. The audience leads; Eva follows.*

> **EVA:** Let's look together!

*They check the watering can. Empty.*

> **LITTLE BUNNY:** Nothing in here!

*They check under the big felt sunflower.*

> **EVA:** Not here either… but ooh, look at this **beetle**.

*A beat of pure distraction. Good — this is dwelling, and it is what an 11-minute runtime is for.*

> **MR RABIT:** *(gently steering, never solving)* Eva — what else is in this garden that is big
> enough to hide a star?

*Eva scans. Camera scans with her, slowly, past the fence, past the pots.*

> **EVA:** Can you see something green?
> **[PAUSE — 3s]**

*Behind the flowerpot, plainly visible to the audience: a green tail, and a row of purple spikes.
Danny is hiding extremely badly.*

> **EVA:** Is that… is that a **tail**?
> **[PAUSE — 3s]**
> **LITTLE BUNNY:** *(whispering, thrilled)* Eva… I think it's wiggling.

*Tiptoeing toward the pot, exaggerated and comic. Music goes playful-sneaky, never scary.*

> **EVA:** *(whisper, to lens)* Should we look behind it?
> **[PAUSE — 3s]**
> **EVA:** Okay… one… two… **three!**

---

### BEAT 10 — SOLUTION (8:30–9:30)

*They peer round the flowerpot. **DANNY** — enormous, entirely visible, crouched behind an object a
quarter of his size, holding the star.*

*He freezes. Caught.*

> **EVA:** Danny!
> **DANNY:** *(sheepish, scuffing one huge foot)* …Hello.

*A real beat. He looks at the star, then at the ground.*

> **DANNY:** I wasn't going to keep it. I just…
> **DANNY:** *(quieter)* I just wanted you to come and find me.

**This is the most important line in the episode.** It states Danny's motive on screen, in his own
voice, in his first appearance — and it is his character forever. Play it small and true. No music
under it.

> **EVA:** *(no scolding — she is delighted)* You could have just **asked**!
> **DANNY:** *(not looking up)* …Could I?
> **EVA:** Of course! Do you want to play with us?
> **DANNY:** *(whole body lifting)* **Really?**
> **LITTLE BUNNY:** *(hopping)* Really really!

*Danny holds out the star in both hands and gives it back himself.*

*Warm sparkle bloom as it passes between them — the discovery sound, the first time a child hears
it. The star **pulses**… then **glows**.*

> **EVA:** *(awed)* Look! It's **glowing**!
> **MR RABIT:** *(kneeling, warm)* That means the adventure is finished, Eva. And a good one.

---

### BEAT 11 — REINFORCEMENT (9:30–10:15)

> **EVA:** *(to the lens, glowing star held up)* We did it — **together!**
> **LITTLE BUNNY:** Together!
> **DANNY:** *(shyly, joining)* …Together?
> **EVA:** Together!

*The four of them, staggered in depth — never side by side at matched scale, per the silhouette
rule. Danny at the back because he is biggest; Little Bunny forward because she is smallest.*

> **EVA:** And do you know the best part?
> **EVA:** We couldn't have done it **without you.**
> **[PAUSE — 3s]**
> **EVA:** Can you clap for Danny?
> **[PAUSE — 3s]**
> **DANNY:** *(overwhelmed, quietly thrilled)* …They're clapping for **me**.

---

### BEAT 12 — SONG BREAK 2 (10:15–10:35)

*Celebration chant — [theme-v2a-chant.mp3](../../audio/theme-v2a-chant.mp3).*

*All four together in the garden, the star glowing warm between them. Danny is in the middle of the
group, not at the edge. This matters.*

---

### BEAT 13 — GOODBYE (10:35–11:00)

*The Cosy Corner. Green armchair, lamp on, warm and low. Everything winds down — this show is often
watched before sleep.*

*Eva sits with the star in her lap, its glow fading gently to a sleepy dim.*

> **EVA:** *(soft)* That was a **good** adventure.
> **EVA:** Will you come back and have another one with me?
> **[PAUSE — 3s]**
> **EVA:** *(softly, happily)* I hoped you'd say that.

*Goodbye song. All four wave. Eva waves last, alone in frame, close.*

> **EVA:** Bye bye, little adventurers!

*She places the star on the shelf. It dims to sleep. Warm fade.*

**Button:** *"Adventure starts with YOU!"*

## 6. THE KEY PHRASE

**"Come on, little adventurers!"** — three uses minimum, per rule 7:

1. 2:25 — the call to go outside
2. 6:45 — leading the search *(as "Let's look together!", the sanctioned search variant)*
3. 10:55 — "Bye bye, little adventurers!" as the sign-off variant

## 7. CONSISTENCY CHECKS

- [ ] Eva wears the pink heart backpack in **every** shot — never removed in this story
- [ ] Danny visibly the biggest in every shot he shares; Little Bunny visibly the smallest
- [ ] Mr Rabit and Little Bunny never side by side at matched scale in matched poses
- [ ] Mr Rabit in spectacles, green jacket, mustard waistcoat, purple bow tie throughout
- [ ] Little Bunny's bow on her **left** ear in every shot
- [ ] Danny's spikes soft-tipped; his grin never reads as a snarl
- [ ] **No whiskers on Eva in any frame**; **no visible teeth on anyone but Danny**
- [ ] No shot under 2.5s; no dialogue shot under 4s
- [ ] Camera at character eye level in every shot without exception
- [ ] Eva's face ≥25% of frame height in all direct-address moments
- [ ] Every question followed by its full pause, no cut during the pause
- [ ] The empty wall stays visible through Song Break 1
- [ ] Nothing sharp in the mix; discovery gets the warm sparkle bloom
- [ ] Danny gives the star back himself and is centre-group by beat 12
- [ ] Ends calm — last 25 seconds wind down
