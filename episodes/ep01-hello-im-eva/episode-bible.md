## 1. WHAT THIS EPISODE IS FOR

This is the pilot, and its job is **attachment, not teaching.** As a FUN episode it carries no
learning objective and must not be given one.

It has four jobs, in priority order:

1. **Make a child love Eva.** Everything else is secondary.
2. **Establish the contract** — *you are not watching Eva, you are going with her.* The child is
   addressed, asked, waited for, and thanked, within the first 40 seconds.
3. **Introduce the Adventure Star** as the show's ritual object.
4. **Introduce the other three** — Little Bunny, Mr Rabit, Danny — each with their signature move,
   so a returning child recognises them instantly in episode 2.

**Logline:** Eva wants to show her new friends her Adventure Star — but it's not on its shelf, and
only the children watching saw where it went.

---

## 2. WHY THIS STORY FOR EPISODE 1

The star goes missing in the very first episode because that is the fastest way to make an object
matter. A child learns what the Adventure Star *is* by watching everyone care that it's gone.

It also establishes the show's central pleasure on day one: **the audience knows before Eva does.**
The child sees Danny take the star at 0:58. Eva doesn't find out until 1:40. For forty seconds the
child is the only one in the room who knows — and they will shout. That shouting is the product.

Per master bible rule 4, nothing is lost or broken: Danny hides the star, he does not damage it, and
he gives it back himself.

## 4. BEAT-BY-BEAT

### Beat 2 — INTRODUCTION (0:20)

Eva alone in her room, centred, straight on, slightly low. Face fills 30% of frame.

> **EVA:** Hello! I'm Eva.
> *(beat — she waits, she blinks, she's genuinely pleased you came)*
> **EVA:** I'm so happy you're here today.

She turns, and the camera follows her look to the shelf. The Adventure Star sits there, soft and
unlit.

> **EVA:** Look — this is my Adventure Star. It's the most special thing I have.
> **EVA:** When it glows… it means an adventure is starting!

**Direction:** Eva says "Adventure Star" slowly and clearly. This is the first time a child hears
the name of an object they will see in every episode for years. Do not rush it.

### Beat 3 — PARTICIPATION (0:38)

> **EVA:** Can you wave hello?
> **[PAUSE — 3 seconds. Eva on screen, facing lens, expectant. Music thins to one held note.]**
> **EVA:** *(delighted)* I saw that! Hello!

Then the call, for the first time in the show's history:

> **EVA:** Come on, little adventurers! Let's go outside!

### Beat 4 — ADVENTURE (0:53)

Room to garden. Little Bunny hops in — her whole body expressing joy, her signature.

> **LITTLE BUNNY:** Eva! Eva! Come and see the flowers!

Mr Rabit is at the low fence, kneeling to their height as he always does.

> **MR RABIT:** Good morning, you two. And good morning to all our friends watching.

**★ THE "THEY KNOW FIRST" MOMENT — 0:58.** Eva sets the star down on the low garden wall and turns
away to the flowers. Behind her, Danny tiptoes in on enormous feet, hiding behind a flowerpot far
too small for him, a spike caught on a leaf. He takes the star. He grins straight down the lens —
delighted, conspiratorial, not remotely menacing — and creeps off.

**Hold this for a full 2 seconds.** The child must register it completely. No music sting that warns
Eva. Danny's comic cue plays, and it is funny, not ominous.

### Beat 5 — CHALLENGE (1:28)

> **EVA:** Little Bunny, come and see my Adventure St—
> *(she turns. The wall is empty. Her ears drop.)*
> **EVA:** …Oh. Where is it?

**Critical direction:** Eva is *puzzled*, not distressed. She does not cry, does not panic. Her
brows go up, not down. A toddler reads fear instantly and this is the pilot.

> **LITTLE BUNNY:** It was right here!
> **EVA:** *(looking at the audience, remembering they're there)* Uh-oh… Danny!

**Eva's wrong guess — required by master bible rule 5.** She looks in the obvious wrong place first:

> **EVA:** Maybe it rolled under the fence… *(looks)* …no. It's not there.

### Beat 6 — THINKING MOMENT (1:53)

Mr Rabit kneels. He does **not** know where the star is, and he must not appear to.

> **MR RABIT:** When something is lost, Eva, what do we do?
> **MR RABIT:** We stop… *(open palm)* …we think… *(finger to temple)* …and we look!
> *(hand shading eyes)*

**Direction:** slow, one gesture per word, held long enough that a child can copy it. This is the
show's catechism and this is its first performance. Get it right here and it is right for 20 episodes.

> **EVA:** *(to the lens, close, low)* Can you help me? Did you see where it went?
> **[PAUSE — 3 seconds. No cut. Eva leans in slightly, listening.]**
> **EVA:** *(lighting up)* Behind the flowerpot? Let's look together!

### Beat 7 — SOLUTION (2:18)

They look behind the flowerpot. Danny is there — hiding badly, enormous, entirely visible, holding
the star.

**Danny's soft moment — required in every episode he appears in:**

> **DANNY:** *(caught, sheepish, scuffing one foot)* I… I just wanted you to come and find me.
> **EVA:** *(no scolding — she is delighted)* You could have just asked! Do you want to play with us?
> **DANNY:** *(whole body lifting)* Really?

He hands the star back himself. Warm sparkle bloom as it passes between them — the discovery sound,
the first time the child hears it. The star pulses, then **glows**.

> **EVA:** Look! It's glowing! That means the adventure was today — and you were in it!

### Beat 8 — REINFORCEMENT (2:38)

> **EVA:** We did it — together!
> **LITTLE BUNNY:** Together!
> **EVA:** We couldn't have done it without you.

The four of them together, staggered in depth — never side by side at matched scale, per the
silhouette rule.

### Beat 9 — GOODBYE (2:53)

Cosy Corner. Lamp on, warm, low. Everything winds down — this is often watched before sleep.

> **EVA:** Will you come back and have another adventure with me?
> **[PAUSE — 2.5 seconds]**
> **EVA:** *(softly, happily)* I hoped you'd say that.

Goodbye song. All four wave. Eva waves last, alone in frame, close.

> **EVA:** Bye bye, little adventurers!

The star settles to a soft sleepy dim on its shelf. Warm fade.

**Button:** *"Adventure starts with YOU!"*

## 5. INTERACTIVE MOMENTS

Five, inside the 4–6 budget. Every one takes the full pause.

| # | Time | Question | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:38 | "Can you wave hello?" | Physical |
| 2 | 1:15 | "Can you see the butterfly?" | Spotting |
| 3 | 2:03 | "Can you help me? Did you see where it went?" | **The big one** |
| 4 | 2:30 | "Can you clap for Danny?" | Physical, celebratory |
| 5 | 2:56 | "Will you come back?" | Relationship |

Moment 3 is the episode. Everything before it is setup for it and everything after is payoff. If it
is rushed in the edit, the episode has failed regardless of how good the rest looks.

- [ ] Eva wears the pink heart backpack in **every** shot — it is never removed in this story
- [ ] Danny is visibly the biggest in every shot he shares; Little Bunny visibly the smallest
- [ ] Mr Rabit and Little Bunny never side by side at matched scale in matched poses

- [ ] Camera at character eye level in every shot without exception
- [ ] Eva's face ≥25% of frame height in all five direct-address moments

- [ ] Nothing sharp in the sound mix; discovery gets the warm sparkle bloom
